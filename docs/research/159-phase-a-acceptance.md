## Corrections and simplifications

- At a smooth zero level of `D`, the `H_D` term drops out. Then `grad rho=0`, `H_rho=grad D grad Dᵀ/epsilon` and `H_sigma=-grad D grad Dᵀ/(epsilon² ln 2)`. The resulting Hessians have rank at most one. The calculus requires actual underlying derivatives. For example, `D(x)=a+abs(x)` with `a>0` still gives a cusp in the regularized ruler at zero. See `hasFDerivAt_fderiv_ruler_on_zero` and `hasFDerivAt_fderiv_scale_on_zero` in [RulerDifferential](../../formal/FractalSync/RulerDifferential.lean).
- Equation 78a is a Shore-only shorthand. The exact necessary inequality includes the wall difference and signed work. Budget sufficiency depends on reaching the crest, a separate trajectory question involving direction, geometry and controls. At an impulse, use the jump law before resuming the smooth ledger. Its linear term can have either sign.
- A radial inward wall covector can produce nonradial acceleration. The inverse metric mixes directions when `g` and `c` are neither parallel nor orthogonal. See `wall_acceleration` in [PotentialForces](../../formal/FractalSync/PotentialForces.lean).
